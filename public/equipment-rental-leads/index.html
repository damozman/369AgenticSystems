What Ava Can Handle

Every Machine on Your Yard, By Unit

Ava works from your own fleet list — whatever is on it. These are the categories equipment and heavy machinery yards typically run. Each machine is tracked as its own asset, not as a category, so “is the mini ex free Monday?” gets a real answer rather than a general one.

🚜

Earthmoving

Excavators and mini excavators, backhoes, skid steers, compact track loaders, wheel loaders, dozers, graders, trenchers.

🏗

Lift & Access

Boom lifts, articulating and telescopic, scissor lifts, telehandlers, rough-terrain and warehouse forklifts, personnel lifts, scaffolding.

⚙

Compaction & Paving

Ride-on and walk-behind rollers, plate compactors, rammers, asphalt saws, screeds, and the attachments that go with them.

⚡

Power, Air & Pumps

Towable and portable generators, light towers, air compressors, welders, trash and submersible pumps, heaters and dryers.

🔨

Concrete & Site Work

Mixers, power trowels, concrete saws and breakers, augers, stump grinders, chippers, pressure washers, skid-steer attachments.

🛻

Transport & Trailers

Equipment and dump trailers, tilt decks, water trucks, delivery and pickup scheduling, and the return date that frees the machine.

A wide fleet is exactly why guessing is dangerous. “Have you got a lift?” could mean a scissor lift, a boom lift or a telehandler, and they are not interchangeable on a job site. Ava asks which one rather than picking. And a machine rented Monday to Friday is held all five days, so it never shows as free on Wednesday while it is sitting on somebody’s site.
