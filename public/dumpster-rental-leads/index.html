What Ava Can Handle

Every Container and Unit on Your Yard

Ava works from your own fleet list — whatever is on it. These are the categories dumpster and portable sanitation yards typically run, and each size and unit type is tracked separately, so “have you got a 20-yard for Thursday?” gets a real answer.

🗑

Roll-Off Dumpsters

10, 15, 20, 30 and 40 yard containers, low-profile driveway-safe boxes, concrete and heavy-debris boxes, swap-outs and extra hauls.

🚻

Portable Restrooms

Standard units, deluxe flushing units, ADA-accessible units, high-rise and crane-lift units, winterised units, event-grade units.

🚿

Restroom Trailers

Two, four, eight and ten stall trailers, climate controlled, luxury and wedding-grade, shower trailers — tracked as individual assets.

🧼

Sanitation Support

Handwash stations, hand sanitiser stands, holding tanks, grey-water tanks, servicing and pump-out schedules.

🏗

Site Support

Temporary fencing and barricades, job-site storage containers, office trailers, wheel-wash and matting where you carry it.

📅

Hire Periods

Daily, weekly, monthly and open-ended hires, scheduled swap-outs, extensions, and end-of-hire collection dates.

This is the niche multi-day hire was built for. A seven-day container hire is not a drop-off appointment — that unit is out for seven days, and Ava holds it for all seven. Ask for the same box on day three and she says it is on hire and offers the next open date, instead of promising a container that is sitting on somebody else’s site.
