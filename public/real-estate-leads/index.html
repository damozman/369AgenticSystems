Where the Pipeline Stalls

Portal Lead Cold Window

Zillow, Realtor.com, and MLS buyers contact 3–5 agents simultaneously. The first to respond wins. Most agents don't even see the notification for 2+ hours. That's 2+ closings per month walking out the door.

Showing Scheduling Chaos

Back-and-forth texts and emails to coordinate showings consume 2+ hours per day. Buyers get frustrated, find another agent, or go direct to the listing agent while you're playing calendar tag.

Follow-Up Sequences That Die

Most leads don't convert on the first contact, and manual follow-up quietly stops after a try or two. The Pipeline Velocity AOS follows up at day 0, 3, and 7 so no lead gets dropped after one try.
