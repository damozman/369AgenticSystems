## BEFORE YOU SEND — PERSONALIZATION CHECKLIST

For every email, know these before hitting send:
- [ ] Practice name (not just doctor name)
- [ ] Doctor's first name (search their website / Google Maps)
- [ ] City (for SEO line)
- [ ] Their domain (for audit hook)
- [ ] Audit score if you ran Gumloop on them (security + SEO)

The audit hook (Variation 1) is your highest converter. Run a Gumloop scan
on their domain before emailing whenever possible.

## SENDING STRATEGY

**Volume:** 10-15 initial emails per day. Don't blast — quality targeting matters.

**Sequence timing:**
- Day 1: Send initial email
- Day 3: Send Follow-Up 1 (if no reply)
- Day 8: Send Follow-Up 2 (if no reply)
- Day 9+: Move to next prospect. Don't chase beyond 3 touches.

**Which variation to use:**
| Situation | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| You ran a Gumloop scan on their domain | Variation 1 (Audit Hook) |
| Larger practice, 3+ providers | Variation 2 (Staff Time) |
| Practice with active Google presence, review mentions wait times | Variation 3 (Response Hook) |
| You have no data and it's a cold name | Variation 2 (safest default) |

**Tracking (Google Sheet columns):**
`Date Sent | First Name | Practice Name | Domain | Email | Variation | Follow-Up 1 Sent | Follow-Up 2 Sent | Reply? | Outcome | Notes`

**Reply handling:**
- Interested → book the call immediately, send cal.com link
- Not interested → reply "Understood, appreciate the response" and mark closed
- No reply after 3 touches → move on, don't follow up again for 90 days

## WHAT TO DO ON THE CALL (Quick Reference)

When they book:
1. **First 2 min:** "Tell me about your practice — how many providers, roughly how many patients?"
2. **Next 3 min:** "What does patient communication look like for your front desk right now? What takes the most time?"
3. **Next 5 min:** Pull up the dashboard demo. Show a live pending response card. Walk through: email arrives → draft appears → you click authorize → patient gets a response.
4. **Last 5 min:** Pricing. $3,000 setup, $1,500/month. "We set it up in one day. First month is the proof of concept — if it's not saving you time, we'll refund."
5. **Close:** "Do you want to move forward?" If yes → send DocuSign MSA same day.
