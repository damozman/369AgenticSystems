# Your Practice. Running Itself.

369 Agentic Systems deploys an autonomous AI agent inside your dental
practice that handles patient email communication — so your front desk
can focus on patients in the chair, not patients in the inbox.

---

## What It Does

**Receives** patient emails (insurance questions, appointment requests,
post-treatment follow-ups, new patient inquiries)

**Reads** their Dentrix patient record — appointment history, treatment
plan, insurance provider, last visit

**Drafts** a personalized, professional response in seconds

**Waits** for your approval — nothing sends without a human saying yes

**Sends** from your practice email address, seamlessly
