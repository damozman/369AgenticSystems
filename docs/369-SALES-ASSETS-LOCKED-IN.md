### 2. Discovery Call Script
**File:** `discovery-call-script.md`
**Status:** Ready to use. Practice it out loud 2-3 times before Monday.

**Structure (15-20 minutes):**
1. **Open (0-1 min):** Warm opener, ask permission to ask questions
2. **Discovery (1-6 min):** 4 questions to surface their actual pain
3. **Transition (6-7 min):** Connect their pain to your solution
4. **Demo (7-13 min):** Screen share dashboard, walk through a scenario
5. **Close (13-18 min):** Price, handle objections, get commitment

**What to practice:**
- The open (say it naturally, like you mean it)
- The discovery questions (listen more than you talk)
- The demo walkthrough (you've done this, it's muscle memory)
- The close (don't ask "what do you think?" — let them respond to price)

**Red flags to catch:**
- If they say "it's fine," probe deeper with the "new patient email after hours" question
- If they say "we already have someone," pivot to "how much time does it take them?"
- If they want to "think about it," ask what specifically they need to think through

**Objection table at end:** Memorize the top 3 (price, AI quality, "send me info")

## WHAT NOT TO DO (Common Mistakes)

❌ **Don't wait for Dentrix to build the integration before selling**
- Your system works without it (Phase 3 core is enough)
- Dentrix integration is a "coming next week" feature in your pitch
- Sell now, integrate later, deliver faster

❌ **Don't mix cold email variations**
- Send all Variation 2 for one week, then A/B test Variation 1 next week
- Mixing them makes it impossible to tell what's working

❌ **Don't use mass mail tools**
- Send from Gmail, one at a time
- It takes 45 seconds per email but converts WAY better
- Mass mail tools get lower open/reply rates

❌ **Don't go off-script on calls**
- The script is tested (Claude in VS did the work)
- Stick to the structure: Open → Discovery → Demo → Close
- Don't pitch until you understand their pain

❌ **Don't negotiate on early customers**
- Price is $3K + $1.5K/month
- The value is there (ROI math in pricing doc proves it)
- If they push back, use the ROI math, don't drop price

❌ **Don't let MSAs sit unsigned**
- They said yes on the call
- Send DocuSign same-day
- Push for signature within 48 hours
- Delays kill momentum
