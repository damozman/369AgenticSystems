### 2. PAYMENT TERMS

**2.1 Subscription Fee**
Client agrees to pay a monthly subscription fee per the tier selected in Exhibit A. There is no
setup fee.

**2.2 Billing**
Fees are billed monthly in advance, beginning on the go-live date. Payment by credit card or ACH.
Invoices unpaid after 15 days accrue 1.5% monthly interest.

**2.3 Price Changes**
Provider may adjust the subscription fee with 60 days written notice. Client may terminate
without penalty if they do not accept the new rate.

**2.4 Usage**
Subscription fees are flat monthly amounts. Provider does not currently meter or bill for call
volume. Should usage-based charges be introduced, they will be disclosed in writing with at least
60 days notice before taking effect, and Client may terminate without penalty.

---

### 3. TERM AND TERMINATION

**3.1 Term**
Month-to-month from the Effective Date. No minimum commitment.

**3.2 Termination by Client**
Client may terminate with 30 days written notice to chris@369agenticsystems.com.

**3.3 Termination by Provider**
Provider may terminate with 30 days written notice, or immediately if Client violates Section 5
(Acceptable Use) or Section 6 (Confidentiality), or fails to pay after 30 days past due.

**3.4 Effect of Termination**
Upon termination, Provider will export and deliver Client's call, lead and booking data in CSV
format within 14 business days. Client's access to the platform and the assigned phone number
will be discontinued on the termination date.
