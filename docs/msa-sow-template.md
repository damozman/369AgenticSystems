### 2. PAYMENT TERMS

**2.1 Setup Fee**
Client agrees to pay a one-time setup fee of **$3,000.00 USD** due upon
signing this Agreement. Services will not commence until setup fee is received.

**2.2 Monthly Retainer**
Client agrees to pay a monthly retainer of **$1,500.00 USD**, due on the
same calendar day each month beginning 30 days after the go-live date.

**2.3 Payment Method**
Payment via ACH bank transfer or credit card. Provider will issue invoices
via email. Invoices unpaid after 15 days accrue 1.5% monthly interest.

**2.4 Price Changes**
Provider may adjust monthly retainer with 60 days written notice.
Client may terminate without penalty if they do not accept the new rate.

---

### 3. TERM AND TERMINATION

**3.1 Term**
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues month-to-month
following the initial 30-day onboarding period.

**3.2 Termination by Client**
Client may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice to
chris@369agenticsystems.com. Setup fees are non-refundable after go-live.

**3.3 Termination by Provider**
Provider may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice, or
immediately if Client violates Section 5 (Acceptable Use) or Section 6
(Confidentiality), or fails to pay after 30 days past due.

**3.4 Effect of Termination**
Upon termination, Provider will export and deliver Client's data in CSV
format within 14 business days. Client's access to the platform will be
discontinued on the termination date.

---

### 4. SATISFACTION GUARANTEE

If, within the first 30 days following go-live, the system fails to
demonstrably reduce patient email response time or free front-desk staff
time, Provider will refund the setup fee upon Client's written request.
This guarantee does not apply if Client has not actively used the approval
workflow for at least 10 business days during the 30-day period.

---

### 5. ACCEPTABLE USE

Client agrees the platform will be used only for lawful patient
communication at their dental practice. Client will not:
- Use the system to send unsolicited commercial messages
- Attempt to reverse-engineer or copy the underlying technology
- Share login credentials with parties outside the practice

---

### 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to keep confidential any non-public business
information shared in connection with this Agreement. This obligation
survives termination for a period of 2 years.

**Patient Data:** Provider will access Client's Dentrix system solely to
retrieve patient context for drafting email responses. Provider will not
store, sell, or use patient data for any purpose beyond service delivery.
Client is responsible for ensuring their use of patient data complies
with applicable HIPAA regulations.

---

### 7. HIPAA ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Client acknowledges that 369 Agentic Systems functions as a Business
Associate under HIPAA to the extent it accesses Protected Health
Information (PHI). Provider agrees to:
- Use PHI only to perform services under this Agreement
- Not disclose PHI to any third party without Client authorization
- Implement reasonable safeguards to protect PHI
- Report any suspected PHI breach to Client within 72 hours

*Note: A formal Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is available upon
request and is recommended for practices with strict compliance requirements.*

### SCOPE OF SERVICES

**1. Onboarding & Setup (One-Time)**
- [ ] Connect to Client's email inbox (read incoming, send outgoing)
- [ ] Integrate with Client's Dentrix system (patient record access)
- [ ] Configure AI response drafting for Client's practice
- [ ] Set up approval dashboard and staff accounts
- [ ] Conduct 30-minute staff training (virtual)
- [ ] Go-live verification test (send and approve one test response)

**2. Ongoing Services (Monthly)**
- Unlimited AI email draft generation for inbound patient emails
- Real-time Dentrix patient record lookup on each incoming email
- Approval dashboard with notification system
- Response delivery via Resend (from Client's practice email domain)
- Full audit log of all drafted, approved, and sent responses
- System monitoring and uptime maintenance
- Support via email (chris@369agenticsystems.com), response within 4 business hours

**3. Supported Email Types**
- New patient inquiries
- Appointment scheduling and rescheduling requests
- Insurance and pre-authorization questions
- Post-treatment follow-up questions
- General practice information requests
- Billing and payment questions

**4. Not Included in Scope**
- Phone call handling
- Social media management
- Marketing campaigns or newsletters
- Integration with software other than Dentrix (Eaglesoft, Open Dental, etc.)
  *(available as add-on — contact Provider for pricing)*
- Legal or clinical advice in patient responses

---

### CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Client agrees to:
- Provide Dentrix API credentials within 3 business days of signing
- Provide email inbox access credentials within 3 business days of signing
- Designate one staff member as primary point of contact
- Ensure designated approver reviews pending responses within 4 business hours
  during normal business hours (response time goals require timely approvals)
- Notify Provider of any patient complaints related to AI-drafted responses
